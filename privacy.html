NHL AUTO POST - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

NHL AUTO POST ("the App") is a personal tool that runs on the owner's own computer. It lets the owner schedule their own videos and send them to their own TikTok account (as drafts in the TikTok inbox) through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

Information the App accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile information (display name and account ID) via the user.info.basic scope, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos to the connected account via the video.upload scope.
  • The videos and captions that the owner chooses to schedule.

How information is stored

All data, including TikTok access and refresh tokens, videos, captions and schedules, is stored locally on the owner's own computer. Tokens are stored encrypted. The App has no external server and does not send data anywhere except to TikTok's official API (and other platforms the owner chooses to connect).

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose any information to third parties. The App is not offered to the general public and has no other users.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time inside the App ("Disconnect") or by removing the App's access in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services), which revokes the stored tokens.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
